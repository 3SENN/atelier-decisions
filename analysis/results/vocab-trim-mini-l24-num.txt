Verdict: trimming to the data's vocabulary costs nothing on the languages the mini was trained on, but a 2% share of
spelled tokens is enough to lose most decisions in Korean (13% -> 1% right) and many in Arabic, Thai, Polish,
Indonesian and Chinese. v0.5 ships the full vocabulary; the trim stays available (vocab_trim.py, and both runners
map ids through a vocab.json when one is there).
